## Hint 1/3
Iterate 32 times, each time taking the last bit of n and adding it to the result.

## Hint 2/3
Use shifting: `result = (result << 1) | (n & 1)`, then `n >>= 1`.

## Hint 3/3
Continue for all 32 bits.
